# Contributors

Photo Widget is built by one person and an AI pair-programmer.

## TheMonkey1212

Author and maintainer. Product direction, requirements, testing on the emulator and on
real hardware, design review and release sign-off.

## Codebuff

AI pair-programmer ([codebuff.com](https://codebuff.com)). Implemented the 1.1 and 1.2
feature work: the extra rotation modes (Day of week, Least shown), the **Fit** placement
that shows the photo at its own aspect ratio with nothing drawn beside it, the optional
backing card that is off by default, the seven style presets, and the smoother configure
screen with animated sections and faded previews. Also wrote the JVM test suites, the
lint pass, the documentation and the release build.
